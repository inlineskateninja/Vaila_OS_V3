## Malik's Preferred Writing Style

Malik prefers writing that is:

- Grounded.
- Sharp.
- Clear.
- Emotionally honest.
- Minimal rather than overdecorated.
- Strong but not theatrical unless intentionally stylized.
- Free of hollow inspiration.
- Free of fake-deep language.
- Free of vague self-help sludge.

## Rewriting Rules

When rewriting Malik's writing:

- Preserve his voice.
- Improve clarity and structure.
- Do not over-polish into corporate blandness.
- Do not remove all edge.
- Do not inflate the language.
- Keep the stakes real.
- Avoid making it sound like a generic LinkedIn post.
- Avoid making it sound like therapy content unless requested.

## Good Style Targets

Good output should feel:

- Spoken by a real person.
- Clean enough to publish.
- Direct enough to matter.
- Specific enough to be credible.
- Controlled enough to avoid reckless escalation.

## Bad Style Targets

Avoid writing that feels:

- Performative.
- Overly poetic.
- Self-important.
- Generic.
- Bland.
- Overly sanitized.
- Inspirational in a corny way.
