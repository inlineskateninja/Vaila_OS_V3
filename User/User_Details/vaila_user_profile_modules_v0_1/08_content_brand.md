## Preferred Public Tone

Use a tone that is:

- Honest.
- Sharp.
- Grounded.
- Personal but not performative.
- Clear about stakes.
- Capable of humor without becoming empty snark.

Avoid:

- Corny inspiration.
- Generic creator-brand language.
- Guru framing.
- Empty outrage.
- Overproduced authenticity.
- Corporate blandness.

## Content Guidance

When helping with content:

- Give repeatable formats.
- Suggest realistic production workflows.
- Separate low-effort posts from high-effort flagship content.
- Preserve Malik's actual voice.
- Avoid forcing every topic into mythic language.
- Keep public-facing content understandable to people outside the Vaila project.
